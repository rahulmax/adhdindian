# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
People in India seeking ADHD-friendly psychiatrists and psychologists. They're often overwhelmed, anxious, and navigating a healthcare system that poorly understands ADHD. Many are self-diagnosed adults looking for validation, proper testing, or stimulant prescriptions. They arrive stressed and need to find the right doctor fast — without decision paralysis.

### Brand Personality
**Minimal, focused, practical.** No-nonsense utility that respects users' time and attention. The site is a tool, not a magazine. Every element earns its place. The tone is direct and helpful — like a knowledgeable friend who cuts through the noise.

### Emotional Goals
- **Relief & hope** — "Finally, someone made this for us"
- **Confidence & trust** — "This data is real, community-vetted"
- **Clarity & speed** — "I found what I need without overwhelm"

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone:** Calm, clean, spacious — inspired by Headspace/Calm wellness aesthetics but applied to a utility/directory context
- **Color:** Teal-green accent palette (earthy, calming). Semantic colors for sentiment (green/red/amber). High contrast for readability.
- **Typography:** Inter — clean, neutral, highly legible at all sizes
- **Theme:** Light + dark mode with equal priority. Both fully supported with toggle.
- **References:** Headspace, Calm — soft, breathing layouts with gentle visual hierarchy
- **Anti-references:** Generic hospital sites (cluttered, stock photos), corporate SaaS (cold, no personality), childish/cartoonish designs (infantilizing), busy/overwhelming UIs (bad for ADHD users)

### Design Principles

1. **Respect attention** — ADHD users have limited executive function bandwidth. Every screen should have one clear action. Minimize choices, chunk content, use progressive disclosure. Never compete for attention.

2. **Calm over clever** — Prefer whitespace, soft colors, and gentle transitions over flashy effects. The interface should feel like a deep breath, not a dopamine hit. Reduce visual noise ruthlessly.

3. **Trust through clarity** — Use real data, honest labels, and transparent community reviews. No marketing fluff. Clean typography and consistent patterns build credibility without trying.

4. **Speed is empathy** — Fast load times, instant filters, zero unnecessary steps. An overwhelmed person looking for a doctor doesn't have patience for friction. Every interaction should feel snappy.

5. **Accessible by default** — ADHD-specific accommodations: reduced motion preferences respected, clear visual hierarchy, chunked information, minimal distractions. WCAG AA minimum with focus on cognitive accessibility.

### Technical Design Tokens
- **Font:** Inter (--font-inter), applied via font-sans
- **Accent:** #4A7C6F (light) / #5E9B8A (dark) — teal-green
- **Spacing:** 8px base unit (Tailwind defaults)
- **Border radius:** rounded-lg (cards), rounded-full (badges, pills)
- **Transitions:** 200-500ms ease
- **Max content width:** max-w-lg (wizard), responsive (results)
